= Fermer

== Understand the basics

Once a meeting is over, the meeting author can close it. This allows to adds the meeting minutes, the notes
that gives a summary on what was discussed during the meeting, the agreements reached, decisions made, etc.

Cela permet d’apporter de la transparence à la réunion et sert également de compte rendu des différentes réunions.

It's also possible to add other kind of metadata to the meeting, such as the organizations who attended, how many attendees
and contributions there were, and what proposals were discussed.

== Configuration

=== Close a meeting

==== Réunions officielles

To close a meeting as an administrator, click the image:icons/action_close.png[Close button] "Close" icon.

image::components/meetings/close/form_backend.png[Close meeting form in the backend]

==== Réunions de citoyens

Tout comme les participants peuvent créer des réunions, ils peuvent également clôturer une réunion. They have fewer fields to fill than administrators.

image::components/meetings/close/button_frontend.png[Close meeting button in the frontend]

Once the meeting has finished, the author of the meeting can see a "Close meeting" button. By clicking on it, they are redirected to
the closing form.

image::components/meetings/close/form_frontend.png[Close meeting form in the frontend]

=== Related proposals

If you link a proposal to a meeting when closing it, the meeting also appears in the proposal page.

image::components/meetings/close/related_meeting.png[Related meeting in a proposal]

This allows participants to navigate quickly in a proposal journey.
