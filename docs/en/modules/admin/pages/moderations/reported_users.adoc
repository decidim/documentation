= Reported participants

There are cases where participants could go against the Terms of Service of the
platform, so it's useful to have the option for reporting and blocking participants.
A participant can be blocked from the xref:participants/participants.adoc[participants
section in the admin panel] or through the "Reported participants" section.

== Report a participant

Any logged in participant can report another participant through the "Flag" icon in the participant
profile.

image:moderations/moderations_reported_participant_flag.png[Report flag in participant profile]

After a participant has clicked in this action they need to provide a reason why they are making this report:

image:moderations/reported_participant_modal_user.png[Give reason for reporting a participant modal]

When an administrator or a moderator report a participant, they have additionnal options that allows them to 
directly block the user and hide all their contents. They will have to give a justification for blocking this participant. 

image:moderations/reported_participant_modal_admin.png[Give reason for reporting a participant modal administrators]

== Manage reported participants

All the reported participants are listed on the reported participant list in the administration panel, with: 
* a link to the participant profile
* the name and nickname of the participant
* the reason why they were reported
* the justification of the report if there is one (by hovering over the reason)
* the date of the report
* the report count (shows if several participants reported the same person)

image:moderations/moderations_backend_list_users.png[Global moderations panel for users]

Administrators can choose to perform two different actions:
* Unreport the participant, if the report is not justified
* Block the participant, if the report is justified

Administrators need to provide a justification if they want to block a participant. 

image:moderations/moderations_participants_block_user_justification.png[Justificate block user action]

Once a blocked participant tries to log-in they will see an error message:

image:moderations/moderations_participants_block_user_alert.png[Message shown to blocked user]

After a participant was blocked its name is changed to "Blocked user".
